*Registration tutorial*

Before you can use the key tracking features, a Gym AG member must register you in the bot\.

1\. Send this command to the bot:
/mytelegramid

2\. The bot will reply with your numeric Telegram user ID\.
This is different from your Telegram username, such as @username\.

3\. Send this information to an authorized Gym AG member:
\- Telegram user ID
\- First and last name
\- Room number
\- Telegram username
\- Phone number, if you want to share it
>If you do not have a Telegram username:
>1\. Open Telegram settings\.
>2\. Go to _"Account"_\.
>3\. Select _"Username"_\.
>4\. Enter an available username and save it\.

4\. The Gym AG member will add you to the bot database\.

After registration, clear bot history with /clear or in the chat settings to remove all the messages\. You are good to go\!

Press on /tutorials to return to the list of tutorials\.
