*Returning a key to a mailbox tutorial*

1\. Press _"Return key to mailbox"_\.
2\. The bot will show the key and it's corresponding mailbox room number\.
3\. Put the physical key into the mailbox\.
4\. Press _"Key returned"_\.
5\. Press _"Confirm"_ only after the key is inside the mailbox\.

After confirmation, the bot records the mailbox as the current holder of the key\.

Important:
\- Do not confirm before the key is physically inside the mailbox\.
\- If you opened this flow by mistake, press _"Cancel"_\.
