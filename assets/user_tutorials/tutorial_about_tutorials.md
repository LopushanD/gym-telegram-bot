Bot tutorials

Use these commands when you need help with a specific part of the bot:

/regestrationtutorial
How to register in the bot\.

/getkeytutorial
How to receive a key from mailboxes or from another person\.

/givekeytutorial
How to hand your key over to another person\.

/returnkeytutorial
How to return your key to a mailbox\.

Press on /start to get the starting message and buttons\.
