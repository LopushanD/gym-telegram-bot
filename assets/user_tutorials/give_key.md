*Giving a key tutorial*

1\. Press _"Hand key over"_\.
2\. Give the physical key to the next member\.
3\. Ask the next member to open the bot and press _"Got key from member"_\.
4\. The next member selects the number written on the key\.
5\. The next member presses _"Confirm"_ after receiving the physical key\.

After the receiver confirms, the bot records them as the new key holder\.

>Important:
>\- Do not start a handover unless you are actually giving away the physical key\.
>\- The receiver must confirm before the handover expires\.
>\- If the handover expires you can simply start a new one\.
>\- Until the receiver confirms, you are still recorded as responsible for the key\.
>\- If you started a handover by mistake, press _"Cancel"_\.
